Q1. Select the word which means the same as the group of words given.
A person who abandons his religion
A) Apostate
B) Prostate
C) Profane
D) Agnostic

Answer: A
Explanation: An apostate is someone who forsakes his religion or principles.

Q2. Choose the correct synonym of the given word.
ABUNDANT
A) Plentiful
B) Scarce
C) Sufficient
D) Meagre

Answer: A
Explanation: Abundant means existing or available in large quantities; plentiful.

Q3. Select the most appropriate idiom for the given situation.
To cross swords
A) To fight or argue
B) To defend someone
C) To kill someone
D) To cross a road safely

Answer: A
Explanation: 'To cross swords' means to have an argument or a dispute with someone.

Q4. Find the correct antonym of the given word.
BARREN
A) Fertile
B) Empty
C) Vacant
D) Clear

Answer: A
Explanation: Barren means too poor to produce much or any vegetation; the opposite is fertile.

Q5. Select the correctly spelt word.
A) Accommodate
B) Acommodate
C) Accomodate
D) Acomodate

Answer: A
Explanation: 'Accommodate' is the correct spelling with double 'c' and double 'm'.
